# Painted City

A living watercolour wallpaper for your Mac. It paints your city live from OpenStreetMap, with the real sun and weather. People, buses, ferries and trains move through it, and windows light up at dusk. Pick any place in the world and choose a painting style: watercolours, gouache, coloured pencil, screen print and more.

## Install

1. **[Download PaintedCity.zip](https://github.com/iamfedese/painted-city-releases/releases/latest/download/PaintedCity.zip)**: always the newest version.
2. Unzip it and drag **PaintedCity** to your Applications folder.
3. **First launch only:** macOS says it can't verify the developer, because this is a hobby project and not from the App Store.
   - **macOS 14:** right-click the app and choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
   - **macOS 15 and later:** open it once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**.

Needs macOS 14 or newer, on an Apple Silicon or Intel Mac.

## Use

Painted City lives in the menu bar (no Dock icon). From its menu:

- **Location:** search for any place, or pick a recent one.
- **Theme:** the painting style.
- **Time of Day / Weather:** live, or fixed to golden hour, rain, snow…
- **Camera Angle, Hills, Style:** how the city is framed.
- **Launch at Login:** keep it running.

It's light on the battery: about 2% of the GPU.

## Updates

The app checks for new versions once a day, downloads them in the background and installs them the next time it quits or the Mac restarts. You can also use **Check for Updates…** in the menu.
